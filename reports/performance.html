What's working, what's not
Working
Email

Follow-up beats the opener

Test 03's step 2 gets an 85.3% open rate and 3.7% reply rate — the best of any step, more than double the opener's reply rate. Don't cut it.

Working
Email

Auto-nurture catches repliers

The 5-step reply-triggered subsequence is live on both active campaigns — responders get a tailored follow-up automatically.

Working
Email

Sending infrastructure is healthy

All 5 mailboxes sit at a 100 warmup score. Whatever's limiting reply rate, it isn't inbox placement.

Watch
Email

The breakup step is dead weight, not steps 5–6

Now that Test 03 has run its full 7 steps, steps 5–6 are still pulling opens and replies — it's step 7 (breakup) that flatlines at zero replies from its first 8 sends. Watch that email's copy specifically.

Fix
Email

Test 02's list needs cleaning

A 40.8% bounce rate means well over a third of that list was undeliverable. Its reply rate looks strong on paper but is running on a contaminated sample.

Opportunity
Email + LinkedIn

LinkedIn beats email on reply rate

Kakiyo's 5.0% reply rate beats email's ~1.8% blended rate on a fraction of the volume, converting mostly senior marketing/creative owners. Invite volume is now near the top of the list (94.6% of prospects invited) — the next lever is growing the list itself.
