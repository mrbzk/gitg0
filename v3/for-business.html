Frequently Asked Questions

What business owners ask before joining.

Is gittgo just another networking group?+

No. gittgo is built around exclusive local positions, trusted businesses, intelligent business introductions and customer opportunities generated through gittgo concierge.

What does one business per category mean?+

Only one business representing each agreed category can hold that position within a defined local gittgo100 area.

Is the exclusivity national?+

No. The exclusivity applies within the member's specific gittgo100 geographical area.

Do I have to attend every event?+

No. The events are designed to be valuable and focused rather than frequent or compulsory. Regular attendance will naturally help members build stronger relationships and gain more value.

Do I have to provide referrals?+

No. There are no forced referral targets.

How are members reviewed?+

Each business is considered before being offered a position, helping to protect the quality and trust within the network.

How does Concierge work for gittgo100 members?+

When a suitable customer enquiry arises within the member's category and local area, that member is gittgo's first call.

Can Connect members receive Concierge enquiries?+

Yes. Connect members may receive suitable Concierge opportunities where relevant.

What is the difference between gittgo100 and gittgo connect?+

gittgo100 is the flagship local membership built around in-person events, a protected local category position and first-call Concierge status. gittgo connect is the flexible online option for businesses that cannot participate fully in a local club.

Can I cancel?+

Membership is paid monthly and can be cancelled in accordance with the membership terms.
